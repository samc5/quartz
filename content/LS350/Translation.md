
Some ideas
- if it's a weird word in Spanish it should be a weird word in English
- how do you translate slang? Consider Johnny's slang in El Perseguidor, which is by necessity a made up dialect
- if a less verbose form doesn't exist in Spanish but does in English (and vice versa) you can probably switch it out (some pages in Spanish are half a page in English)
- a literal translation is not a bad starting point
- Robert Croll sometimes leaves Spanish bits from the original in his English translations
- There is always something fundamentally different about a translation even if it's done well and doesn't necessarily lose anything
- strangeness should be left intact
	- Borges uses many words to say the wrong thing, etc

Robert Croll on Idioms
- often you can use an idiom that does the same work
- But sometimes the idioms will be grounded in a certain context
	- in this case you kind of just make up an idiom still grounded in that context
