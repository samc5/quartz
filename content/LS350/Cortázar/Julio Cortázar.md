Raised in Argentina, moved to France pretty young

His father abandoned his family when he was 6. He read a lot as a child

He became a high school teacher and professor in Argentina, and wrote regularly

In 1951 (age 37) he moved to France

He was pretty pacifist and socialist, and supported the Sandinistas in Nicaragua as well Castro and especially Che in Cuba (note that Che Guevara was also mentioned extensively in the short video we watched in class about the political climate of Argentina in the wake of Evita´s death). Was also anti-peronist which caused some problems in the 40s

Was exiled from Argentina for around the last decade of his life

Died in Paris in 1984

Wrote Rayuela (Hopscotch), which is a unique text that you can read through either in order by chapter or in a specific order that he lays out
